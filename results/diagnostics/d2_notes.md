## Reading of this table, written before D2 ran

Reading of this table. 13.8 reads a change below the seed SD as 'the model did not use the input'. The table separates skill from outputs and prints those words only where the outputs support them.

Skill, from the histograms of boot_rule7.npz: pooled AP and AUROC against their 81-based SDs (ejido 0.0124, 0.0069; sq8km 0.0180, 0.0116); AP at equal FPR per fold, which no per-fold offset can move, against its SD (0.0112; 0.0247); the mean of fold APs against its SD (0.0123; 0.0152); each fold's AP change against that fold's SD over the baseline's seeds 0 to 2 (ejido 0.0360, 0.0168, 0.0078, 0.0280; sq8km 0.0554, 0.0197, 0.0077, 0.0140; two degrees of freedom each); and the pooled AP change that each fold's median logit change would give alone.

Outputs, from the two prob_oof.tif, every 4th pixel, the AP's pixels: per fold the mean |dP|, the share changing side of the threshold, the median logit change (offset), its robust SD, the Spearman correlation and the overlap of the top k pixels, k being the positives of the set. Yardsticks: two baseline seeds give mean |dP| 0.011 to 0.032, Spearman 0.43 to 0.67, overlap 0.68 to 0.83; numerical differences alone (CPU against GPU, same weights and input) give at most 0.0005, at least 0.9994, at least 0.994.

State: the share of training in the blank's state. E1b: 4.83 to 11.93 % of cells, none inside sq8km. E2: both masks 0 on 0.0017 to 0.0045 % of pixels.

'Did not use the input' is printed only if pooled AP and AUROC are below their SDs, no fold is beyond its SD, and every fold has mean |dP| below 0.001, Spearman at least 0.999 and overlap at least 0.99. If the outputs moved and skill stays below the SDs, the row says 'uses the input, no measurable loss of skill without it'. Otherwise it says 'uses the input or what it learned from its masks; removal changes skill', and the fold columns say where.

## What the blank is (checked before D2 ran, CPU, on windows; scripts and logs in `../review_logs/d2_blank/`)

What the blank is for E2. D2 sets all eight added channels to 0. Training did not hold that state over a window. The NDVI-texture mask is 0 on 10,918, 21,670, 8,704 and 17,408 of the 332,136,448, 349,962,240, 353,107,968 and 377,487,360 training pixels of folds 1 to 4 (0.0025 to 0.0062 %), on at most 678 pixels of any tile and on no positive pixel. The PAN mask is 0 on 64 to 72 % of training pixels, but it equals Neo NDVI >= 0.3 on 99.99 % of cached pixels, so on a vegetated pixel it is 0 only 20,601 times in 170,561,925 (0.012 %). Both masks are 0 together on 5,777, 15,738, 7,544 and 13,612 training pixels. So the NDVI mask is a constant 1 in training and its conv1 filter works as an offset: 1.05, 0.90, 0.64 and 0.83 in conv1 output RMS per fold, where the six value layers together give 0.85, 1.27, 1.11 and 1.05 on all held-out tiles; that is 0.39, 0.30, 0.29 and 0.35 of bn1's running SD, up to 1.3 SD in a single channel. The blank takes this offset away and sets the PAN mask to 0 on vegetated pixels, beside setting the values to their mean.

Size check on windows, not the D2 number: CPU, float32, AP without CIRCUNVALACION. Four windows of 3,840 px hold 92 % of the pixels and 88 % of the positives of sq8km; with four more of 3,072 px the windows hold 17 % of the pixels and 24 % of the positives of the ejido. On the self-test's window this CPU blank moves P by 0.1071 (E2) and 0.0291 (E1b), against 0.1072 and 0.0294 on the GPU. Inside sq8km, E2, folds 1 to 4, AP as run 0.6215, 0.6762, 0.7005, 0.6846. The blank changes it by +0.0383, -0.1179, -0.0841, -0.1227. The NDVI mask alone at 0, all texture kept: +0.0090, -0.0036, +0.0186, +0.0004 in the folds and +0.0413 pooled, with 62 to 86 % of the blank's mean |dP|. Values at the training mean, masks real: -0.0140, -0.0260, -0.0574, -0.0729. The true tensor from 1,024 m away: -0.0835, -0.1504, -0.1535, -0.1282. So for E2 the sign in fold 1 and the size in folds 2 to 4 depend on how the input is removed, and the pooled AP moves by more than the seed SD of 0.0180 with no texture removed at all. D2's change for E2 cannot be read as what the texture values contribute, whether it is small or large.

What the blank is for E1b. Its state does occur in training: no PlanetScope date on 9.75, 11.93, 7.24 and 4.83 % of the training cells of folds 1 to 4, and on at least 99 % of 98, 120, 76 and 45 of the 1,267, 1,335, 1,347 and 1,440 training tiles. It occurs only where PlanetScope does not reach. None of the 161, 138, 120 and 133 training tiles with at least half their area in sq8km has such a cell, so inside the centre D2 asks the model for something it saw only elsewhere. Those cells hold fewer positives: 1.77, 1.23, 0.37 and 2.72 % against 5.74, 5.78, 4.91 and 6.46 % elsewhere, ratios of 3.2, 4.7, 13.1 and 2.4. Size check on windows holding 92 % of sq8km's pixels, CPU, not the D2 number: under the blank the mean P falls by factors of 2.2, 7.1, 8.3 and 1.7; with the values at their training mean and availability real, by 1.6, 1.8, 1.6 and 1.2. AP without CIRCUNVALACION, as run 0.6406, 0.8182, 0.7384, 0.7176: blank -0.0319, -0.1818, -0.1982, -0.0509; values at the training mean, availability real -0.0311, -0.1324, -0.0612, -0.0645; true tensor from 1,024 m away -0.0635, -0.1699, -0.0821, -0.0742. So a fall under the blank includes the model's response to a no-data state it learned on peripheral land, and does not by itself say how much the model needs PlanetScope's content.

Cancellation. The table's flags read the pooled change, which can hide what happens inside the folds in two ways: folds of opposite sign, and a change of score scale that differs between folds. A logit offset of 1 in one fold, with no pixel re-ranked inside any fold, moves the pooled AP without CIRCUNVALACION of E2 by -0.0403 to +0.0297 inside sq8km and by -0.0184 to +0.0044 in the ejido, and of E1b by -0.0372 to +0.0284 and by -0.0257 to +0.0121, where the seed SDs are 0.0180 and 0.0124. Size check on windows, not the D2 number. E2's blank raises the median logit by 1.90, 0.79, 0.63 and 0.67 in folds 1 to 4 inside sq8km; these offsets alone move the pooled AP of the stored runs by +0.0352 there, and the ejido's offsets (1.84, 0.93, 0.65, 0.76) move it by -0.0173. Inside sq8km the blank's pooled AP change is -0.0284, which is 1.6 SD, while the folds change by +0.0383, -0.1179, -0.0841, -0.1227 (mean -0.0716, 4.7 times the 0.0152 SD of that mean) and the AP at equal FPR by -0.0476. No pooled AP change fell below the seed SD on these windows: E2 -0.0284 in sq8km and -0.0801 in the ejido, E1b -0.1283 and -0.1692. The pooled AUROC did: E2's blank on the ejido windows, which hold 17 % of its pixels, changes it by +0.0063 against an SD of 0.0069, while the folds change by +0.0331, -0.0394, -0.0280, -0.0112, each beyond that fold's SD over the baseline's seeds (0.0196, 0.0074, 0.0119, 0.0054).

The seed SD is also not the noise of this comparison. It measures differences between trainings. D2 compares the same weights, where even CPU and GPU arithmetic differ by at most 0.0009 in a window's AP and 0.0008 in its AUROC. Two seeds of the baseline, whose whole models differ, have a Spearman correlation of 0.43 to 0.67, a top-k overlap of 0.68 to 0.83 and a mean |dP| of 0.011 to 0.032 per fold. So a change below the seed SD says that removing the input costs less skill than retraining varies. It does not say that the model did not use the input.

## Suggestion for Shafi

Suggestion for Shafi, not done. Keep D2 as 13.8 writes it and add one arm: the same weights with the added values at the training mean (normalised 0) and the masks or availability as they are. It is not needed to keep the words 'did not use the input' from being applied wrongly; the columns of this table do that. It is needed to say what takes their place, because no column can separate the values from the other changes the blank makes: in E2 the removal of the NDVI-mask offset and a PAN mask at 0 on vegetated pixels, in E1b the no-date state and its lower share of positives. Size check on windows holding 92 % of sq8km, CPU, AP without CIRCUNVALACION, by fold: E2 blank +0.0383, -0.1179, -0.0841, -0.1227 against the arm -0.0140, -0.0260, -0.0574, -0.0729; E1b blank -0.0319, -0.1818, -0.1982, -0.0509 against the arm -0.0311, -0.1324, -0.0612, -0.0645. On a wider sample, 17 % of the ejido, the arm rises in fold 1 (E2 +0.0310, E1b +0.0221), so it too is read fold by fold. It is not a neutral state for E1b: the branch output at mean input lies 0.078 to 0.120 (RMS per channel) from the mean branch output on data, whose spatial RMS is 0.097 to 0.160; the blank's lies 0.083 to 0.185 from it. Cost, from the stored times of these runs, one job on the GPU at a time: E2 inference 534.2 s (129.4, 149.7, 135.0, 120.1), with scoring 823.6 s; E1b inference 1,440.1 s (578.5, 329.0, 296.9, 235.7), with scoring 1,664.4 s; both 1,974.3 s (32.9 min) of inference and 2,488.0 s (41.5 min) in the queue. Most of E1b's time is reading PlanetScope for each window, which the arm needs for availability; the baseline's inference takes 225.3 s. It also takes 2.4 GB of rasters and, on the CPU, 1,931 s of rescoring for the ejido and sq8km, 2,451 s with the B4 tiles. If only one experiment is run, it is E2, whose blank is outside training over a whole window.
