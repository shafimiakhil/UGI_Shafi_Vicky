## What can and cannot be claimed, statement by statement

### (a) 'The model did not use the input': does not hold

They say that the outputs depend on the input. They do not say that the input helps: the readings stay no gain, and no size is read as the importance of a sensor (13.4, rule 8). E1 has no D2; its case rests on D1 and the own arms below.
