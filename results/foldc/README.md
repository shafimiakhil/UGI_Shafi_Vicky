**What the gap rests on.** Five polygons hold 56.2 % of the 5,322,273 positives: 3048 (CANTERO, 967,840 px: 572,452 in Q1, 395,388 in Q2), 3049 (CIUDAD UNIV, 854,893 px inside the square, all in Q1), 3224 (RIO, 591,286 px), 3226 (RIO, 336,645 px), 3225 (RIO, 242,560 px) (`review_logs/f02_polygons.log`). They stay in the reference (13.5); this concerns how to read the scores. AP is a sum over positive pixels, so at fixed rankings the weighted gap of 0.0524 splits exactly: 3048 +0.0201, 3049 +0.0157, the three RIO polygons -0.0018 together (3226 +0.0074, 3224 -0.0041, 3225 -0.0050), the other 698 polygons +0.0184, positive in every seed (0.0241, 0.0165, 0.0146) (`review_logs/f03_contrib.log`, `review_logs/contrib_by_polygon.csv`). With 3048 and 3049 left out of scoring on both sides, the three gaps fall to 0.0144, 0.0157, 0.0148 (by seed number from -0.0088 to 0.0496), their block intervals include zero, and whole-square AUROC reverses, 0.9489 for fold C against 0.9444 (`review_logs/f02_polygons.log`, `review_logs/f07_block_interval.log`). Those two are a selected case, the polygons on which the quadrants lead most. With the five largest left out, a rule that does not look at the direction, the gaps are 0.0574, 0.0551, 0.0529, at least 0.0410 in every seed, block intervals 0.0270 to 0.0873, 0.0209 to 0.0850, 0.0197 to 0.0823 (`review_logs/f03_contrib.log`, `review_logs/f07_block_interval.log`). What differs most is where the tiles lie: the quadrant models train on 177, 147, 131, 142 tiles touching the centre, which hold 14 to 19 % of the UGI pixels of their training tiles (2.60 to 3.41 M); fold C on none, its nearest training tile 552 m from the square (`review_logs/f04_amounts.log`, `review_logs/f10_checks.log`). The positive-weighted gap is 0.0257 (by seed 0.0314, 0.0554, -0.0097) where fold C's tiles are nearer by more than 1 km (14.5 % of the positives) and 0.1419 (0.1722, 0.1438, 0.1098) where they are farther by more than 2 km (13.8 %) (`review_logs/f08_distance.log`). **Thresholds and checkpoints.** Thresholds from internal validation, by seed: 0.14, 0.155, 0.085. Best epoch and internal-validation AP, by seed: 39, 0.6654; 45, 0.6825; 50, 0.6644.

Object recall is on the same 679 polygons in both columns, of the 703 with a pixel inside the square. A polygon counts in a run when at least half of its pixels are evaluated in that run (`itareo/ugi_seg/score.py:198`), and it is scored once, on those pixels. In the four quadrants a polygon crossing a quadrant line (14 of the 679, among them 3048, 3224 and 3226) is scored once, by the model of the quarter holding at least half of it, on its part in that quarter; no polygon counts in two quadrants. So 35 polygons are scored on part of their pixels on the right and 22 in fold C, those 22 being cut by the edge of the square and also among the 35 (`review_logs/f06_objects.log`).

Caveat of 13.7: the centre is enclosed by training data on all sides (non-held tiles in the buffer's row or column span: 204 north, 289 south, 272 west, 204 east; `review_logs/f04_amounts.log`), hence the buffer. "Fewer positives" concerns the scored set, not the training: 5,322,273 positive pixels in 81 blocks here, against 25,178,583 in 625 blocks for AP without CIRCUNVALACION in the ejido (`review_logs/f12_ejido_count.log`), so the block intervals are wider, 0.1324 for fold C and 0.1149 for the quadrants against 0.0724 (`summary_foldc_sq8km.json`, `summary_centre_sq8km.json`, `summary_baseline_ejido.json`); the two columns score the same positives and still differ in width. Pixels outside the ejido stay in the loss, as in the sensor series (13.6); tiles straddling a quadrant line stay out, because the cache does not hold them: 57 of them lie outside the buffer, with 682,306 UGI pixels (`review_logs/f04_amounts.log`).
